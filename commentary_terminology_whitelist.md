# Broadcast terminology whitelist

This whitelist records observed English-language broadcast usage. It is not a
dialogue table and contains no proposed Volley World Manager lines.

## Validated terms and constrained uses

| Term | Classification | Supported use | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|---|
| `dime` / `dimed` | TERM VALIDATED | Excellent reception/pass. S7–S9 provide three independent retained examples; the expanded search repeats this usage. | Do not use as evidence for a high-quality set. Do not expand it to `platform dime`. |
| `funnel` / `funneling` | TERM VALIDATED | Analyst description of block-defense shaping or channeling an attack toward a defender/zone. S11 and S13 are independent examples. | Treat the intentional plan as analyst inference. Do not convert every block touch or forced direction into a funnel. |
| `tool` / `tools the block` | TERM VALIDATED | Hitter intentionally uses the block and sends the ball out or away. | This does not validate the passive phrase `got tooled`. |
| `block touch` | TERM VALIDATED | Ball contacts the block; may be further described as slowing the attack for back-court defense. | A touch does not itself prove intentional direction. |
| `cross-court` / `cross-court shot` | TERM VALIDATED | Diagonal attack direction. | Do not join it to `bullet` without direct evidence. |
| `bullet` | TERM VALIDATED, CONTEXT-LIMITED | Emphatic description of some hard serves and attacks in the expanded search. | The compound `cross-court bullet` was not found. |
| `perfect set` / high-quality-set description | TERM VALIDATED | Qualitative evaluation of a setter salvaging or delivering a ball. | `dime` is not validated as the setter equivalent. |
| `overload` / `combination play` | TERM VALIDATED | Analyst description of multiple attacking routes stressing or splitting blockers. | Visible-pattern evidence alone does not establish a called play; S11/S12 are needed when claiming the huddle instruction was followed. |

## Terms not validated

| Exact term | Classification | Closest observed equivalents |
|---|---|---|
| `platform dime` | TERM NOT VALIDATED | `dime pass`, `passed a dime`, `dimed that ball` |
| `got tooled` | TERM NOT VALIDATED | `tool`, `tools the block`, `off the block`, `off the hands` |
| `soft block` | TERM NOT VALIDATED | `block touch`, `touch`, `off the hands`, `slow it down` |
| `sprawl dig` | TERM NOT VALIDATED | `dig`, `save`, `pancake`, `one-arm stab` |
| `cross-court bullet` | TERM NOT VALIDATED | `cross-court shot` and `bullet` occur independently |
| `beaten by tempo` | TERM NOT VALIDATED | Speed used to beat the block; no time to close the seam |

`TERM NOT VALIDATED` means the exact expression was not found in the 28-match
targeted pool. It does not claim that no English-language commentator has ever
used it.

## Routing implications

- `funnel`, timing causality, overloads, assignments, and called-play effects
  belong primarily to analyst evidence.
- `dime`, `tool`, attack direction, and block touch can occur in PBP or replay
  analysis, but should still pass a salience check.
- Exact percentages, metres, seconds, internal assignment identifiers, and
  requested-versus-achieved numeric values remain `UI / DIAGNOSTIC`.
- A valid term is not a requirement to speak. Routine receptions, digs, and
  block formation remain `OPTIONAL / OFTEN SILENT` where the corpus supports
  silence or compressed calls.
