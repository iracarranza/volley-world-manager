# Part 5 — Career and Player Development

The management loop: what a career owns, how a week changes a player, and how
that change is supposed to become visible on court.

Part 5 is where [P1-C1](../part_01_project/01_what_you_are_building.md)'s
central claim is either honoured or quietly abandoned. A training system that
raises numbers is easy. One whose effects a manager can *see and attribute* is
the actual design problem, and chapter 2 is entirely about it.

   — development projects, the three outputs, and the design test

## The test to carry

> Compare two otherwise identical players either side of a threshold. If the
> only visible difference is a hidden two-percent outcome change, the feature
> needs clearer opportunity or information feedback.
