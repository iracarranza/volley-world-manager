# Academy selection: proof, fit, and exceptional roles

Design record. This document refines academy selection inside the club → regional strength → Sixnet structure.

## 1. Academy places are earned through proof

A regional academy is not a list of the volis who most perfectly resemble the region's tactical ideal.

Selection asks a harder question:

> Which eligible volis have most convincingly demonstrated that they can add value to this region's representative squad?

The primary evidence comes from the club game: playing time, role, performance, opponent quality, consistency, specialist success, and the contexts in which a voli's contribution has held up.

A voli can therefore earn academy selection through more than one route.

## 2. Orthodoxy is one route, not the rule

One common route is near-perfect assimilation into the regional system.

Example: a Blôc libero who has spent years reading the seams deliberately left by the region's tall, patient blocking structure may be the natural first libero even if another eligible libero has stronger isolated digging attributes.

Their value is proven in the exact relationships Blôc expects:

```text
regional block structure
        +
repeated club evidence in those seams
        +
pair / tactical familiarity
        ↓
representative value
```

This is not a hidden 'system fit' bonus. The voli has accumulated evidence that they can perform the relevant job reliably.

## 3. Contrary or specialist existence is another route

A voli can also earn selection by proving that a distinct individual style solves a problem the standard regional system does not solve as well.

Example: Blôc may normally favour tall, patient read blockers, but one commit-blocking specialist has repeatedly shut down quick-heavy offences at club level. That specialist is not automatically selected because commit blocking is rare. They must prove that the exception is strong enough to justify a roster place.

Likewise, a second libero may earn selection because their club evidence shows unusual success against tooling specialists who otherwise exploit the Blôc block.

The selection logic is therefore not:

```text
regional conformity
→ call-up

specialist difference
→ wildcard slot
```

It is:

```text
WHAT VALUE HAVE YOU PROVEN?
        ↓
CAN THAT VALUE HELP THIS ACADEMY SQUAD?
        ↓
DOES IT JUSTIFY A PLACE RELATIVE TO THE OTHER ELIGIBLE VOLIS?
```

A contrary style is not intrinsically more valuable, less valuable, or more interesting than orthodox fit.

## 4. No protected 'wildcard' pathway

Specialists are not owed academy places merely because they are unusual.

A region may have several distinctive club-level specialists and call none of them.

The academy should be able to conclude:

- the orthodox starter is more useful overall;
- the specialist's club success does not translate to likely Sixnet opponents;
- the specialist duplicates a function already covered elsewhere;
- the specialist has not produced enough high-level evidence;
- the tactical adaptation cost is too high for the limited representative preparation window;
- another voli offers a stronger marginal contribution.

Likewise, an orthodox voli is not owed a place merely because they match the regional ideal.

## 5. Selection is about marginal squad value

The academy is assembling a roster, not ranking individuals in a vacuum.

A voli's selection case depends partly on what the rest of the squad already provides.

Example Blôc roster logic:

```text
FIRST LIBERO
excellent seam reader
high familiarity with canonical block/floor relationships

SECOND LIBERO CANDIDATE A
similar seam reader, slightly weaker

SECOND LIBERO CANDIDATE B
less orthodox
elite at reading tool attempts and emergency deflections
```

Candidate B may be selected because they add a capability the first libero does not already cover.

This does not mean B is 'better'. It means B has higher marginal value to this particular representative roster.

The same principle can apply across positions:

- a commit-blocking middle behind two patient read blockers;
- a serving specialist behind more complete outsides;
- a high-control setter behind a more aggressive first setter;
- a defensive opposite who changes late-set matchup options;
- an undersized explosive middle whose quick-attack threat is unlike the region's usual net profile.

## 6. Club competition is the proving ground

The club system matters because it gives unusual volis somewhere to demonstrate that their way of playing actually works.

A voli who does not fit the dominant local pathway can:

```text
find a club that gives them minutes
        ↓
play a role that exposes their actual strengths
        ↓
produce evidence against real opponents
        ↓
force academy selectors to take the contribution seriously
```

This connects club tactical diversity directly to representative selection.

A contrarian club can therefore become an important route into the academy for volis who would otherwise remain bench or reserve players in conventional local clubs.

Cross-regional clubs can serve the same function: a voli may play abroad or under a foreign-like club tactic, prove a distinctive role there, and still return to represent their home region if their evidence is strong enough.

## 7. Tactical fit affects cost, not worth

Academy selectors should care about how quickly a voli can function inside the representative system, especially because academy preparation time is limited.

But tactical alignment should not be a hidden master score.

Relevant distinctions remain:

- **formative inheritance** — what the voli learned as normal;
- **current-system familiarity** — how well they know this actual system;
- **pair familiarity** — how well they know relevant teammates;
- **tactical discipline** — how strongly they adhere to real calls;
- **decision-making** — how well they resolve live situations;
- **proven role value** — what their club evidence says they can contribute.

A low-alignment specialist may still be worth selecting if the contribution is sufficiently large and the role can be integrated cleanly.

A high-alignment voli may still miss out if they have not demonstrated enough value.

## 8. Selection pathways should remain asymmetric

Neither of these pathways is explicitly superior:

```text
ASSIMILATION PATH
learn regional system deeply
perform canonical role repeatedly
become difficult to replace

CONTRARY PATH
preserve or develop a distinct role
find enough club opportunity to prove it
show that the difference solves a real representative problem
```

One may be easier in a particular region, generation, or position.

For example, a Blôc seam-reading libero may have an easier path because the region produces many club situations that showcase that skill. A commit-blocking specialist may need stronger or rarer evidence because selectors are being asked to spend a roster place on something outside the default structure.

But the governing question remains the same:

> How well did this voli prove their worth?

## 9. Selection evidence should have provenance

Do not reduce the selection process to one opaque academy score.

Useful evidence can be separated conceptually:

```text
ROLE EVIDENCE
minutes, responsibility, lineup status

PERFORMANCE EVIDENCE
actual results in the role

CONTEXT EVIDENCE
opponent quality and relevant matchup types

SYSTEM EVIDENCE
performance in roles close to academy expectations

SPECIALIST EVIDENCE
repeatable success in a distinct or contrary function

ROSTER EVIDENCE
what unique value remains after the likely squad is assembled
```

The eventual selection model can summarize these internally, but player-facing explanations should be able to point back to concrete reasons rather than saying 'academy fit 84'.

## 10. Sixnet consequence

This makes the Sixnet a concentrated regional philosophy without turning it into tactical purity.

A representative squad can be recognizably Blôc while carrying a commit-blocking middle and a tool-defence libero. Those volis do not weaken the regional identity merely by being unusual.

Instead they show the region's strongest interpretation of its own volleyball at that moment:

```text
stable regional philosophy
        +
most proven eligible volis
        +
selected specialist answers
        ↓
academy squad
```

The academy is therefore both representative and selective: it embodies the region's fixed philosophy while rewarding individuals who have demonstrated that they can strengthen, extend, or occasionally contradict the default structure effectively.

## 11. Design rule

> Academy selection rewards demonstrated representative value, not conformity.

Some volis prove that value by becoming perfect expressions of the regional system. Others prove it by being useful enough that the region makes room for something different.

Neither route is privileged in principle. The club season is where both claims are tested.
