Six interfaces the design asks for and the build does not have

Aesthetic drafts only — no implementation. Each plate is drawn at the game's window proportion, in the Mikasa palette taken verbatim from ui_palette.gd, set in the game's own two faces.

Every screen here has been through one rule, and the first draft failed it badly:

The interface may state what is known, recorded, observed, scheduled, promised, or said by a named person. It may not tell the player what to conclude, what it means, or what significance it will have later.

Attribution is what makes judgement legal. Physio · 3 notes is a fact about the physio. Three of them look tired is the interface claiming to know. And a heading may name what is under it — never the reader's relationship to it, which is why If she came here became Offer.
