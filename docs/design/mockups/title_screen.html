Mockup · the screen that exists, drawn on purpose

Two scraps and a masthead

The screen keeps the shape it already has — the title stacked down the left, the menu off to the right, the file count at the bottom of the brand column. What changes is that nothing is pasted on top of the page any more. ui_backdrop.gd already draws two taped paper scraps on every screen, and it happens to put them exactly where this screen needs its two cards. The redesign is to stop drawing panels over them and let them be the panels.

The scraps were already there

ui_backdrop.gd anchors its scraps at 0.74,0.10 and 0.06,0.68 of the sheet — the top right and the bottom left. Those are the menu and the continue card. Same anchors, same torn silhouette, same lighter tape patches; only the size changes. The panel that was floating over the top-right scrap is deleted, not restyled.

Why the buttons stopped being buttons

A rounded panel with four gold-barred rows is a menu from a different game laid on this page. The entries are now written in the display face at the size the masthead is written in, and the one you are on is ringed in accent rather than filled — the mark a person makes on a list they are choosing from. Hover moves the ring. Nothing slides.

The numbers are gone

01 NEW CAREER through 04 EXIT numbered a list that is not a sequence: nobody does them in order and there is no third step. Numbering that encodes nothing is decoration wearing the costume of structure. The four entries carry their own weight, and the last one is quieter than the other three because leaving is not the thing you came here to do.

The straight line is the only one

The accent bar across the top stays, and it is now doing a job: it is the single machine-straight edge on a page where the ruling slopes, the scraps are torn, the underline overshoots and the stitches wobble. One printed edge is what makes the rest read as a hand.

Continue is a card, not a fifth entry

The ask was a button in the bottom left that opens the last played save, and the bottom left is where SaveCount already sits. So it goes on the second scrap, above the count, and the count becomes its caption. It is deliberately not in the menu: the menu is where will you go next, and this is where you already were. list_save_metadata() sorts by last_saved_unix descending, so the save it opens is saves[0] — no new field, no new file read.

Open · Leave the desk

04 EXIT is drawn here as Leave the desk, which is a proposal and not a fix-in-passing. Every other label in this game names an object or an act; the one that ends the session currently names a system call. Say the word and it goes in, or stays EXIT.
