# Gate 33: Setter Decision Progression

Review date: 2026-07-30

Status: **PASS; SHADOW ONLY**

The paired setter fixture was rerun after moving selection behind the
observation boundary. It holds serves and outgoing passes fixed while changing
only perception, movement, and setting attributes.

Across 600 responses per tier, developing, established, and elite setters
remained monotonic for confidence, reachability, action count, controlled sets,
and jump sets. Mean confidence progressed from 0.443 to 0.548 to 0.655; mean
action count progressed from 0.145 to 0.950 to 2.154; true reachability
progressed from 3.07% to 11.40% to 53.07%. There were zero invalid fixtures and
zero paired outgoing-flight mismatches.

This gate does not grant a hidden speed or reach bonus. Better setters recognize
the pass more accurately and retain more genuinely executable choices.
