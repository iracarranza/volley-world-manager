# Gate 39: Hitter Observation and Progression

Review date: 2026-07-30

Status: **PASS; SHADOW ONLY**

A paired 120-sample fixture varies only assigned hitters' perception, movement,
jump, approach, and attack attributes across developing, established, and elite
tiers.

Mean confidence progresses from 0.566 to 0.670 to 0.775. Perceived reachability
progresses from 37.50% to 76.67% to 100%; true reachability from 0% to 70% to
95.83%; mean perceived actions from 0.375 to 3.692 to 5.000; and executable
contacts from 0% to 49.17% to 95.83%. Every metric is monotonic with zero
invalid fixtures.

No universal movement, jump, contact-height, or reach bonus is applied.
