# Gate 32: Perceived Setter Opportunities

Review date: 2026-07-30

Status: **PASS; SHADOW ONLY**

Second-contact ownership is now ranked entirely from `PlayerObservation`.
The previous selection term based on authoritative `true_arrival_margin` was
removed and replaced with the setter's perceived arrival margin.

True reachability, physical actions, balance, and contact timing remain in the
candidate as resolver and diagnostic evidence. They may reject or degrade a
chosen action, but they cannot retroactively alter which action the player
selected from the information available to them.
